# Demo promise (draft for ISAK to approve)

Draft 19 Nov 2026. This is the contract with ourselves: what the first build for friends contains, and just as important, what it does NOT. Anything not on the "in" list goes to the Later frame on Miro.

## The promise in one sentence

In about 25 minutes you live through the first days of the outbreak over Minneapolis from orbit, you save (or fail) a few people you came to care about by what you saw and said, and you want one more pass.

## In

- **Place:** Minneapolis South Side, inside an Upper Midwest map (the horde comes from the south-east, along the highways from Milwaukee and Chicago). Real streets and buildings from OpenStreetMap.
- **Time:** Act 0 (Launch, normal life, tutorial) through Act 2 (Denial), ending on the first night of Act 3 (Panic). Orbit time compressed: one pass over the region is about 3-4 minutes of play; between passes the game fast-forwards and shows what changed.
- **Passes:** about 6 passes over the region.
- **The view:** three zoom levels with no loading screens: region, city, street (dots).
- **The station:** one window and one console (orbit track, next pass countdown, local time below, radio dial). No station upgrades, no docking.
- **Abilities (level 1 only):** telescope look, thermal at night, tune the radio dial, answer a call, patch two groups, warn a neighbourhood, coach one star live through an escape.
- **People:** dots (simulation), clickable dots become simple person cards, and 3 hand-written stars with real arcs.
- **Radio:** about 20 calls, some true, some exaggerated, at least one lie you can catch with your own eyes.
- **Outcomes:** made it / died waiting / betrayed, shown on the next pass. One ending card that tells you what happened to your stars.
- **Sound:** radio static, voice filter, station hum, placeholder voices.
- **Settings:** volume, text size, subtitles, pause, save and load.

## Out (on purpose)

- Other countries and the globe view (a tiny teaser shot at most).
- Docking, other stations, the skill tree.
- Nukes, the missile hack, spacewalks, Kessler debris.
- Station survival systems (food, water, oxygen). Power limit only if the prototype shows it is fun.
- AI-generated stories at runtime.
- Steam page, achievements, controller support, translations.

## How we know it worked

- Friends finish it without help.
- At least half of them ask "is there more?" or want one more pass.
- They can tell you the name of at least one star afterwards.
